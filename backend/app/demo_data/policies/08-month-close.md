# Month close

Finance closes a month in Budgets after the approved claims for that month are complete.
Aim to close each month by the fifth working day of the next month.
Closing a month records the reason and the person who closed it, and then it freezes the claim lines for that month.
After a month is closed, no claim can add or change its lines for that month.
An AI agent may request a month close, but the close waits for a finance user to confirm it in the browser.
Do not close a month while a claim from that month still waits for a decision, unless the claimant has agreed to carry it forward.
A closed month cannot be reopened, so write the reason with care.
