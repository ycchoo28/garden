# Equipment and software

Equipment that costs less than 150 US dollars for each item can be bought on a claim, with a receipt.
Equipment that costs 150 US dollars or more goes through a purchase request to the operations team before you buy it.
Software licences need your manager's approval before you buy them, even when they cost less than 150 US dollars.
Subscriptions that renew automatically must be listed in the software register with a named owner.
Personal devices used for work are not repaid unless the purchase was approved in advance.
Equipment bought for the studio is recorded on the studio asset list, with its serial number in the line description.
A replacement for broken equipment needs the asset number of the broken item.
