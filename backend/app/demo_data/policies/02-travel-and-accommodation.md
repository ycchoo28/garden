# Travel and accommodation

Business travel must be agreed with your manager before you book when the trip costs more than 400 US dollars in total.
Book economy class for flights shorter than six hours.
Business class needs written approval from your manager and from finance, and it is rare.
Hotels are capped at 220 US dollars a night in most cities and at 300 US dollars a night in the major cities on the finance list.
Book hotels through the company travel desk when you can.
Use rail for trips under four hours within the same country.
Taxis and ride-hailing are fine for trips to and from airports, stations and client sites.
Keep ride receipts that show the pickup time, the route and the fare.
Mileage for a personal car used on company business is repaid at the rate that finance publishes each January.
If your travel runs beyond the planned dates, say why in the description of the line.
