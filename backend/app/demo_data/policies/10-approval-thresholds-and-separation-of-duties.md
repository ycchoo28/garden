# Approval thresholds and separation of duties

A claim above 500 US dollars needs a second approval from finance, after the department manager has approved it.
Finance sets the threshold, and it applies to the total of the claim.
Nobody may approve their own claim, and nobody may decide two steps of the same approval.
A finance user who files a claim is approved by a manager and then by a different finance user.
A knowledge batch that changes the policy library needs approval from an admin, and the person who started the batch may not give it.
AI agents may prepare work and request actions, but they never certify, approve, reject or erase on a person's behalf.
Keep a record of each approver, the date of each decision and the reason for every rejection.
Finance reviews this policy once a year with the finance lead.
