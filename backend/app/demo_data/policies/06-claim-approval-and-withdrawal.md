# Claim approval, rejection and withdrawal

A manager reviews each claim from their team within five working days.
A manager may approve a claim, or reject it with a reason that the claimant can act on.
A rejected claim goes back to the claimant with its reason.
The claimant may correct the lines and submit again, or leave the claim as it is.
A claimant may withdraw a submitted claim before anyone has decided it.
When an AI agent asks to withdraw a claim, the withdrawal waits until the claimant confirms it in the browser.
Every approval is recorded with the name of the approver and the date.
Nobody decides two steps of the same claim.
Finance may ask a manager to explain an approval.
