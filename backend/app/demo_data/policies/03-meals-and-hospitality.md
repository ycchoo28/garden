# Meals and hospitality

Meals while you travel on company business are repaid up to 60 US dollars for each full day, with a receipt for every purchase.
A team meal to mark a project milestone is allowed up to 25 US dollars for each person.
A client meal needs the names of the people who attended, their companies and the business purpose in the line description.
Alcohol is repaid only as part of a client meal, and it must fit inside the meal limit.
Gifts for clients must be agreed in advance and must not exceed 75 US dollars for each person in a year.
Tips are repaid up to 15 percent of the bill.
Personal meals and snacks for personal use are not repaid.
Groceries for the studio kitchen are bought within the weekly budget that the operations team sets, and they are not claimed separately.
