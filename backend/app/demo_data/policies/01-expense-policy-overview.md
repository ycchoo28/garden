# Expense policy overview

This policy explains how people at Fernhill Studio spend company money and how that spend is repaid.
It applies to every employee, manager and finance team member.

## Who this covers

Anyone who pays for work with their own money follows this policy.
Anyone who buys with a company card follows the same categories as everyone else.

## How a claim works

Create a draft claim with a short title that says what the money was for.
Add one line for each receipt, with the date the money was spent, the category, the amount in US dollars and a short description.
Attach a photo or PDF of each receipt to its line.
When the claim is complete, certify it and submit it.
Only the claimant may certify and submit a claim.

## Approval levels

Your department manager approves every claim from your team.
A claim above 500 US dollars also needs a second approval from finance once the manager has approved it.
Nobody may approve their own claim, including managers and finance staff.

## Timing

Submit a claim within 60 days of the date of its earliest line.
Finance closes each month in Budgets, and a closed month never reopens, so talk to finance before you submit a claim for an old month.

## When the rules do not fit

Ask your manager first when a rule does not fit your situation.
If the answer is still unclear, ask finance before you spend the money.
