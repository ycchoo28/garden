# Receipts and records

Every line on a claim needs a receipt, except for tips and mileage, which are described in the line.
A receipt must show the merchant name, the date, the amount paid and the currency.
A photo is acceptable when the text is clear and the whole receipt is in the frame.
Card slips from a company card are not needed on a claim, because the monthly statement already holds them.
If you lose a receipt, say so in the description of the line, and your manager may approve a lost receipt for up to 30 US dollars.
Finance keeps receipts and claims for seven years.
Do not upload personal documents, such as identity cards or bank statements, as receipts.
