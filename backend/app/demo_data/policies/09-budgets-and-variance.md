# Budgets and variance

Finance sets a budget for each department, for each month and for each spend category.
A budget line is one amount in US dollars for one combination of department, month and category.
Replacing a budget line needs a reason, and the old amount stays in the history of the line.
Managers see the budget lines and the variance for their own department only.
Variance is the budget minus the actual spend, so a positive figure means the department is under budget.
Actual spend is the sum of the approved claim lines for the same month and category.
Finance closes a month in Budgets once its claims are complete, and a closed month never reopens.
Review variance with each department manager in the first week of the month.
When you change a budget, the reason should name the decision that caused the change, not only the new amount.
